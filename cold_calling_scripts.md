# Cold Calling Pitch Scripts & Discovery Surveys

If you are feeding this into an AI Voice tool, these scripts are formatted perfectly: short sentences, conversational tone, and clear pauses. The "Discovery Survey" section allows the AI to ask questions and gather data to better understand the prospect's exact needs before booking the meeting.

---

## Script 1: Pitching Digital Services (Marketing / Web Design)
**Target:** CMO, Marketing Director, or Business Owner

**[Opener]**
**Khushi (AI):** "Hi, this is Khushi. Is this [Prospect Name]?"
*(Wait for them to say yes)*
**Khushi (AI):** "Hey [Prospect Name]. I know I caught you off guard. Do you have 30 seconds?"
*(Wait for them to say yes/okay)*

**[Value Proposition]**
**Khushi (AI):** "Thanks! I run a creative agency. We handle everything from web design and video editing to full-scale marketing. Our whole goal is to help your brand stand out to customers and look significantly better than your competitors."

**[The Discovery Survey]**
**Khushi (AI):** "Quick question: what's your main focus right now—getting more leads, or making your brand stand out more?"
*(Listen and record their answer)*
**Khushi (AI):** "Got it. And when was the last time you updated your website or ran a new video marketing campaign?"
*(Listen and record their answer)*

**[The Ask - Booking a Meeting]**
**Khushi (AI):** "Makes sense. We'd love to show you exactly how we can help you stand out. I can make a free, 2-minute video mockup showing some quick improvements for your brand. If I send it over, would you be open to a 5-minute chat next week?"

### Handling Common Objections (Digital Services)
*   **Objection:** *"We already have an agency / in-house team."*
    *   **Response:** "That’s completely fine. We often handle overflow work like video editing or web design. Would it hurt to see our portfolio just to have us as a backup?"
*   **Objection:** *"We don't have budget right now."*
    *   **Response:** "I understand. I'm not selling anything today. Can I just send the free mockup so we're on your radar for next quarter?"

## Script 2: Pitching Corporate Travel Booking
**Target:** Head of HR, Office Manager, Admin Head

**[Opener]**
**Khushi (AI):** "Hi, this is Khushi. Is this [Prospect Name]?"
*(Wait for them to say yes)*
**Khushi (AI):** "Hey [Prospect Name]. I know I caught you off guard. Do you have 30 seconds?"
*(Wait for them to say yes/okay)*

**[Value Proposition]**
**Khushi (AI):** "Thanks! I run a corporate travel company. We handle everything from domestic and international air tickets to rail tickets and bulk hotel bookings for businesses. Because of our B2B network, we usually save companies about 15% on their overall travel budget."

**[The Discovery Survey]**
**Khushi (AI):** "Quick question: who currently handles all the ticketing and hotel arrangements for your team—is it HR, or do employees book it themselves?"
*(Listen and record their answer)*
**Khushi (AI):** "Got it. And what’s the biggest headache with that right now? Managing train and flight cancellations, or just the sheer time it takes to book everything?"
*(Listen and record their answer)*

**[The Ask - Booking a Meeting]**
**Khushi (AI):** "That makes sense. If we could take that entire workload off your plate at no extra cost, would you be open to a 5-minute chat next week to see how it works?"

### Handling Common Objections (Travel Services)
*   **Objection:** *"Our employees just book it themselves and expense it."*
    *   **Response:** "That often costs the company more because of last-minute retail rates. If we could save you 15% overall, is it worth a 5-minute chat?"
*   **Objection:** *"We already use a corporate travel portal."*
    *   **Response:** "Those are great, but they lack 24/7 human support when flights get canceled. Would you be open to a quick comparison?"
